About this tool

Free, no sign-up. Your screenshot is sent once to Google Gemini to read the fonts (this is a third-party call, and it is the exception here - most techtuate tools run fully in your browser). We do not store the image and there is no account. For each font it suggests real alternatives you can actually use, starting with the closest Microsoft Office / Windows font and then free downloadable ones (Google Fonts and other free foundries). Google Fonts preview live; Office and system fonts render in the preview only if you have them installed. Exact font identification is hard even for paid tools, so this leads with the family and closest equivalents rather than overclaiming.
