about this tool

About the business card maker

Free, no sign-up and no watermark. Pick a format, choose one of eight hand-tuned templates and fill in your details. The QR code can save your contact straight to a phone (with only the fields you tick) or open a link. Downloads are a print-ready PDF with vector text, 3 mm bleed and crop marks, a home-print sheet for A4 or Letter card stock, 300 DPI PNGs, or the QR on its own. Everything is made in your browser and your design is remembered in this browser only. The one exception is the optional "digitize your card" scan, which sends the photo once to Cloudflare Workers AI (Google Gemini as backup) to read it and is labeled where you use it. Part of techtuate, a growing collection of little tools that just work. Got a pile of cards from other people? The business card reader turns them into contacts.

questions people ask

Questions people ask

Is this business card maker really free?

Yes. Every template, the print-ready PDF, the home-print sheet and the QR code are free, with no sign-up and no watermark. We don't sell printing, so there is nothing to upsell.

What does the QR code on the card do?

You choose. By default it opens a small contact page with a Save to contacts button, which works with any scanner app. Your details are packed inside the QR itself and nothing is stored on a server. It can also hold a plain vCard for offline use, or open a link such as your website or LinkedIn. You pick exactly which details go into it.

Can I print the cards at home?

Yes. Download the home-print sheet: up to 10 cards on an A4 or Letter page with cut marks, and the backs on a second page lined up for double-sided printing. For a print shop, use the print-ready PDF with bleed and crop marks.
