techtuate vs. CloudConvert

Checked August 2026. CloudConvert is the broad converter; techtuate covers the common conversions locally.

CloudConvert is a genuinely powerful converter that handles a huge range of formats on its servers. For that reach it is the right tool. techtuate does not try to match every format - it converts audio to MP3 or WAV (and lets you trim a clip) and turns SVG into PNG or JPEG, all in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and there is no per-minute cap.

TL;DR

CloudConvert supports hundreds of formats but uploads your file and meters free use by conversion minutes, often with a sign-up for heavier use. techtuate covers the everyday jobs - audio to MP3/WAV with trimming, and SVG to PNG/JPEG at any size with a chosen background - fully in the browser, with no upload and no cap. Use CloudConvert for exotic or bulk conversions; use techtuate for the common ones you want kept local.

What CloudConvert is great at

  • Format breadth. Hundreds of input and output formats, including many techtuate does not do.
  • Server power. Big files and batch jobs run on their infrastructure, not your device.
  • An API for automating conversions in a pipeline.

Where the two differ

  • Upload vs. local. CloudConvert uploads your file to convert it. techtuate decodes and re-encodes in the browser tab, so the file never leaves your device.
  • Caps and sign-up. CloudConvert's free tier is limited by conversion minutes. techtuate has no per-file cap and no account.
  • Extras that pure converters skip. The audio tool trims a clip while it converts; the SVG tool lets you set an exact output size and a transparent, white, or custom background.
  • Scope. For a format techtuate does not support, CloudConvert is the better call - breadth is its whole point.

When to use which

Reach for CloudConvert when you need an unusual format, very large files, batch jobs, or an API.

Reach for techtuate when the job is a common audio or SVG conversion and you would rather keep the file on your device with no cap.
