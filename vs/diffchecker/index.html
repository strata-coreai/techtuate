techtuate vs. Diffchecker.com

Checked August 2026. Both show a side-by-side text diff - this is about what sits around it.

Diffchecker.com is the tool most people land on for "diff checker", and its basic text compare is good. techtuate's diff checker does the same core job - side-by-side and unified views with word-level highlighting - as a clean page with no ads and no account, where nothing is uploaded and you can share a comparison by a link that carries the data itself rather than saving it on a server.

TL;DR

Diffchecker is feature-rich (PDF, image, folder and spreadsheet diffs, saved diffs) but leans on ads and pushes a paid "Pro" plan, and several of those extras upload your files. techtuate keeps it to text, code, and config, entirely in your browser, with ignore-whitespace and ignore-case, a JSON normalize toggle, share-by-link with no server, and a .diff export. Use Diffchecker for its extra file-type diffs and saved history; use techtuate for a fast, ad-free text and code diff that never leaves your device.

What Diffchecker.com is good at

  • More than text. It also diffs PDFs, images, folders, and spreadsheets, which techtuate does not.
  • Saved diffs and history when you make an account, useful for coming back to a comparison later.
  • Well known with a layout many people already recognize.

Where the two differ

  • Nothing uploaded. techtuate compares both sides in your browser, so proprietary code or a config file is not sent to a server. On Diffchecker, the basic text diff runs in the browser, but its file-type diffs and the save/share features involve their servers.
  • No ads, no account. techtuate is just the diff - no ad slots and no "Pro" upsell.
  • Share without a server. techtuate's share link packs both blocks into the URL itself (compressed), so a coworker opens the exact diff instantly and nothing is stored anywhere. The link carries the text, so you share it like you would share the text.
  • Config-friendly toggles. Ignore whitespace, ignore case, and a "Format JSON" normalize that sorts keys so you diff meaning instead of formatting noise.
  • Standard export. Download the differences as a unified .diff / .patch file.
  • Scope. If you need to diff PDFs, images, folders, or Excel files, or keep a saved history, Diffchecker has those and techtuate does not.

When to use which

Reach for Diffchecker.com when you need to compare non-text files (PDF, image, folder, spreadsheet) or want an account with saved diff history.

Reach for techtuate's diff checker when you want a fast, ad-free text or code diff that stays in your browser, with share-by-link and a .diff export.
