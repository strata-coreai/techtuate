techtuate vs. CamScanner

Checked September 2026. Both turn a photo of a page into something that looks scanned - the difference is what you have to install, sign up for, and put up with on the free tier.

CamScanner is the app most people mean when they say "scan it with your phone", and it is genuinely capable: multi-page documents, OCR, cloud sync across devices. But it is an app you install, it wants an account, and on the free tier it stamps a watermark on what you export. techtuate's Photo to Scan is a single web page: open it, drop in a photo you already took, and it finds the page, flattens the perspective, fills in the fingers holding it, and gives you a PNG, JPG or PDF. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and the photo never leaves your device.

TL;DR

Use CamScanner if you scan a lot, need multi-page documents, OCR and text export, or want your scans synced across devices, and you do not mind an account and a subscription to drop the watermark. Use techtuate when you have a photo already, want one clean flat page out of it now, and would rather not install an app, create an account, or export something with a watermark on it.

What CamScanner is good at

  • Multi-page documents. Shoot a stack of pages and get one PDF, reordered and named. techtuate does a single page per export.
  • OCR and text export. It reads the text on the page so you can search or copy it. techtuate does not do OCR at all.
  • Sync and sharing. Scans land in your CamScanner account and follow you to another phone or the desktop app.
  • Capture-time guidance. The camera view helps you frame the page and can auto-shoot when it is steady.

Where the two differ

  • No install, no account. techtuate runs in whatever browser you already have, on a phone or a laptop. CamScanner is an app on the App Store or Play Store and asks you to sign up.
  • No watermark. CamScanner's free tier marks exported pages, and removing that is what the subscription is for - the App Store listing shows in-app purchases at around $9.99 a month and $49.99 to $69.99 a year (checked September 2026). techtuate's export is clean, and free.
  • The photo stays on your device. techtuate does the edge detection, flattening and cleanup in the browser tab, so a passport page or a payslip is never uploaded. CamScanner's model is built around storing your scans in its cloud.
  • No ads. techtuate shows none.
  • Finger removal. techtuate looks for fingers holding the page down and fills them in with the surrounding paper, and gives you a brush for anything it misses. That is the part hand-held photos usually ruin.
  • Scope, honestly. techtuate is one page in, one page out: no multi-page PDFs, no batch, no OCR, no cloud library. If you need those, CamScanner is the better fit.

When to use which

Reach for CamScanner when scanning is a routine part of your week, you need multi-page files, searchable text, or your scans available on every device, and a subscription is worth it to you.

Reach for techtuate's Photo to Scan when you already have the photo, you want one flat, clean, watermark-free page out of it in the next thirty seconds, and you would rather the document not be uploaded anywhere.
