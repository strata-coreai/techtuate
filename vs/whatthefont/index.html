techtuate vs. WhatTheFont

Checked August 2026. Both identify fonts from an image - this is about what you get handed at the end.

MyFonts WhatTheFont is the default answer for "what font is this". It is good at spotting a typeface, and then it points you at the matching font to buy from a foundry catalog. techtuate's font finder identifies the likely family too, but hands you the closest Microsoft Office font and the closest free Google Fonts match, so you can use something right away in Word or Docs.

TL;DR

WhatTheFont matches against a large paid foundry catalog, which is ideal when you want to license the exact font. techtuate leads with usable equivalents: the nearest Office font you probably already have, plus a free Google Fonts match, with no sign-up. Both are best-guess identifiers, not exact-match guarantees. Use the first to buy the real thing, the second to get working immediately for free.

What WhatTheFont is good at

  • A huge catalog. It matches against MyFonts' commercial library, so it is strong at naming a specific commercial typeface.
  • Buy-the-font path. If you need to license the exact font, it takes you straight there.
  • Mature matching engine with a long track record.

Where the two differ

  • What you can do next. techtuate gives the closest Microsoft Office font and the closest free Google Fonts match, so you can finish the design without a purchase. WhatTheFont's suggestions lean toward paid foundry fonts.
  • Cost and sign-up. techtuate is free with no account. The screenshot is sent once to a labeled AI service to read it and is not stored.
  • Honesty about exactness. Both are best guesses. techtuate leads with the family and closest matches rather than pretending to name every custom typeface exactly.

When to use which

Reach for WhatTheFont when you need to license the exact commercial font and money is not the constraint.

Reach for techtuate's font finder when you want a free, usable match now - the nearest Office font plus a free Google Fonts equivalent you can drop into a document.
