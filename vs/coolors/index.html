techtuate vs. Coolors

Checked August 2026. Coolors builds color schemes; techtuate pulls colors out of an image you already have.

Coolors is a superb color-scheme app: generate palettes, tweak them, save them, and build a whole design system. techtuate's color palette extractor does one slice of that job - drop an image, get its dominant colors as HEX and RGB, and copy a single swatch or the whole palette - with no account.

TL;DR

Coolors is the right tool for generating and managing palettes, with an image picker plus a lot more, and it nudges you toward an account for saving and pro features. techtuate is the quick "what colors are in this image" grab: extract, copy, done, no sign-up, nothing uploaded. Use Coolors to design a scheme; use techtuate to lift colors out of a photo fast.

What Coolors is great at

  • Palette generation. Spacebar through schemes, lock colors, adjust harmonies.
  • Saving and organizing. Accounts let you keep and share palettes and projects.
  • A full ecosystem of contrast checkers, gradients, and apps.

Where the two differ

  • Focus. techtuate does image-to-palette extraction only, so there is nothing to learn - drop an image, read the HEX and RGB values.
  • Copying. Copy one swatch or the entire palette in a click; no account to unlock it.
  • Local and quiet. The image is read in your browser tab, not uploaded, with no sign-up nudges.
  • Scope. If you want to generate, refine, and store schemes, Coolors is the deeper tool by design.

When to use which

Reach for Coolors when you are designing a color scheme and want to save and iterate on it.

Reach for techtuate's color palette extractor when you just need the colors from an existing image, fast, with no sign-up.
