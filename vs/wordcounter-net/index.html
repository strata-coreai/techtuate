techtuate vs. WordCounter.net

Checked August 2026. Both count words and characters live - this is about what sits around the counter.

WordCounter.net is the tool most people land on for "word counter", and it does the job. techtuate's word and character counter gives the same core numbers - words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time - as a clean page with no grammar-checker upsell, and your text is counted in the browser rather than sent anywhere.

TL;DR

WordCounter.net is established and adds features like keyword density, but leans on ads and nudges toward writing-tool upsells. techtuate keeps it to the counts people actually want, on one quiet screen, with a character-limit checker and nothing uploaded. Use WordCounter.net for its extra analytics; use techtuate for a fast, no-upsell count that stays on your device.

What WordCounter.net is good at

  • Familiar and complete. Live word and character counts plus keyword density and other analytics.
  • Long track record and a layout most people already know.
  • No sign-up for the basic counter.

Where the two differ

  • No upsell, no ads. techtuate is just the counter - words, characters with and without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and a character-limit check - with nothing pushing a paid writing tool.
  • Your text stays put. Counting happens in your browser, so a draft you would rather not paste into a random site is not sent to a server.
  • Character-limit checker. Set a limit (a tweet, a meta description, a form field) and watch it live.
  • Scope. If you specifically want keyword-density analysis, WordCounter.net has more of that.

When to use which

Reach for WordCounter.net when you want its extra analytics like keyword density.

Reach for techtuate's counter when you want a clean, ad-free live count that stays in your browser, with a character-limit check.
