techtuate vs. CamCard

Pricing and behavior checked July 2026.

CamCard is one of the best-known business card scanners. It is also a contact database that lives on someone else's servers: to use it you make an account, and every card you scan is uploaded and kept in CamCard's cloud so it can sync across devices and sell you a subscription. techtuate's card reader takes a different shape - it reads the card, hands you the contact, and keeps nothing.

TL;DR

CamCard = account required, your scanned contacts stored on their cloud, business plans $21-$25/user/mo. techtuate = no account, no stored server copy, your phonebook stays in your browser, export a vCard or CSV, free.

One honest thing up front

Unlike the rest of techtuate, the card reader does make a single call to a third party (Google Gemini) to read the image, because good card reading needs a strong vision model and we will not pretend a weaker in-browser engine is as accurate. We are upfront about that on the tool. The difference from CamCard is what happens next: we do not keep an account, we do not store the image, and your saved contacts live only in your browser. CamCard's whole model is to hold your contact database on its servers indefinitely.

What you are actually signing up for with CamCard

  • An account, always. Even the free tier requires you to register before you can scan.
  • Your contacts on their cloud. Scanned cards sync to CamCard's servers. That is the feature and the lock-in: your address book now lives with them.
  • A subscription for real use. Business plans run about $21-$25 per user per month, with monthly scan caps on lower tiers.
  • An app install. It is a mobile app first, not a "open a link and go" tool.

What techtuate does instead

  • No account. Open the page and scan. Nothing to register.
  • No stored server copy. The image is sent once to be read and is not saved by us. There is no cloud database of your contacts.
  • Your phonebook is yours. Saved contacts live in this browser (IndexedDB). Export them any time as a vCard (.vcf) that imports into any phone, or as a CSV.
  • No app. Works in the browser on your phone or laptop.
  • Free. No tier, no per-seat pricing.

The honest trade-off

CamCard's cloud sync is genuinely useful if you want a team-wide, always-synced contact database with CRM integrations, and you are comfortable with that data living on their servers and paying per seat for it. If that is you, CamCard is built for it.

techtuate is for the far more common case: you were handed a card, you want it in your phone in ten seconds without an account or an app, and you do not want a subscription or a permanent cloud copy of your contacts. For that, a read-and-export tool is exactly right, and it costs nothing.
