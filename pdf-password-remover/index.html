Free, no sign-up. The unlocking runs entirely in your browser using a WebAssembly build of qpdf, so your PDF and its password never leave your device. That matters here more than anywhere: these are usually bank statements, payslips, and tax certificates, exactly the files you should not be uploading to a random site to "unlock". Questions people ask

How do I remove a password from a PDF?

Choose the PDF, and if it is protected, enter the password you use to open it. The tool returns an unlocked copy. Everything happens in your browser, so the file is never uploaded.

Is it safe to unlock a bank statement or tax PDF online?

With this tool, yes, because the PDF and the password stay in your browser and are never sent to a server, unlike tools that upload your file to unlock it.

Do I need to know the password?

Yes. This removes a password you already have, the one you use to open the file. It cannot crack or recover an unknown password. PDFs that only restrict printing or copying can be unlocked without a password.

Will the unlocked PDF keep its text?

Yes. It removes the encryption while keeping the original pages and selectable text, so it is not flattened into an image the way printing to PDF often is.
